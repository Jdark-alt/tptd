# Siegeline v19.2 spec: Wave planner layout

Oct 22, 2026 · @Jerry

## What changes and why

This is a layout and clarity pass on the attacker's Wave step. The rules from v19.1 stay. Only where things sit, how big they are and how they're explained changes. The Wave step is the attacker's main screen after unit design, so it must be obvious what to touch first.

- **Master time slider and play button** become the most prominent controls on the screen.
- **Spread track** goes back to full width, with a visible knob at *both* ends. The start knob is missing today.
- **Path strip** moves from beside the spread track to **under** it, full width and much slimmer.
- **Sabotage rows** use the same two controls: one release tick and one dot on the path.
- A clearly visible **"How to use"** button opens short, step-by-step instructions.
- Build on `bridges` and label it v19.2. No protocol or balance changes. Note any choices this spec doesn't make in the `CLAUDE.md` entry.

- The order is fixed: title bar, presets, master panel, histogram, route label, then one block per design, then one block per sapper.
- Each design block = header line, then spread track, then path strip right under it. The gap between blocks (16 px) must be larger than the gap inside a block (4 px), so each pair clearly belongs together.
- Spread tracks and path strips all start and end at the same x positions, so bands in different blocks line up vertically.
- "busiest second" and the route length stay as they are today, just placed as above.
- On phones the same column is used; nothing changes except widths.

## Master slider and play button

These are the first things a player should notice. They sit together in their own panel, with a 1 px accent-coloured border and a slightly lighter background than the rest of the step.

### Look

- **Play button:** a 56 px circle filled with the accent orange, with a dark play or pause icon. It's the only filled circle on the screen.
- **Until the player first presses play or moves the slider in a match:** the play button pulses softly (a glow ring every 2 s), and a line under the slider reads "Press play, or drag the dot, to watch your wave move." Both stop for good after the first touch.
- **T readout:** "T = 17.2 s" in 28 px bold next to the play button. It's the largest text in the Wave step.
- **Slider track:** 8 px tall. The part left of the thumb is filled orange; the rest is grey.
- **Thumb:** 28 px circle, orange with a white ring. Touch area at least 44 px.
- **Ticks:** labels at 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 s. A thin vertical marker at 20 s labelled "releases end".

### Behaviour (must work exactly like this)

- **Play:** moves T forward at real speed (1 s per second) from where it is now, updating the map, every spread track and every path strip each frame.
- **Pause:** stops T where it is. The icon swaps between play and pause.
- **At 60 s:** playback stops and shows the play icon. Pressing play again restarts from 0 s.
- **Dragging the slider, or dragging anything on a spread track or path strip, pauses playback first.**
- **Keyboard:** the space bar toggles play and pause while the Wave step is open and no text field has focus.
- **Leaving the Wave step pauses** playback. Coming back keeps the same T.
- Test the button on a fresh match, after editing the army, and after switching the bridge toggle. It must work in all three.

## Each group block

Each design gets one block: a header line, then a tall spread track (when units leave), then a slim path strip (where they are at T). The two controls edit the same timing, so changing one moves the other right away.

### Header line

- Design icon and name in its own colour, 16 px bold: "Runner".
- Then in 12 px grey: "×6 · leaves 11.0–15.0 s".
- Selecting a block (tap the header) gives it the accent border, as Brute has in the current build.

### Spread track (when units leave)

- **Full widget width**, 0 s at the left edge and 20 s at the right. About 56 px tall, so individual unit dots can still be dragged as today.
- Small time labels under the track: 0, 5, 10, 15, 20 s.
- The release band has **two matching knobs**, one at each end: 10 px wide, 24 px tall, the design's colour, with a white grip line. Fix the missing start knob.
- **Drag the start knob** to change when the first unit leaves. **Drag the end knob** to change when the last unit leaves. The other knob stays put.
- **Drag the band's middle** to slide the whole group earlier or later, spread unchanged.
- Unit dots sit inside the band as now and can still be dragged one by one.
- When T is between 0 and 20 s, draw a thin vertical line on the track at T, so you can see who has already left.

### Path strip (where they are at T)

- **Directly under its spread track, full widget width, only 16 px tall.** Keep today's look: a grey route line, the group's band in its colour, a tick per unit.
- Left edge = entry, right edge = keep. The whole strip is the whole route at T, so the band's length is in proportion to the route. Example: a group whose front and rear are 10 cells apart on a 20-cell route fills half the strip. Check the current build does this; fix it if not.
- Shaded stretches show where the route is inside tower range; a small "B" marks a planned bridge tile.
- Units still waiting show as "+3" at the left edge; units at the keep show as "×4" at the right edge.
- **Drag the band** along the strip to choose where the group is at T. The release time on the spread track above moves to match. Limits and toasts stay exactly as in v19.1.
- Print "Entry" and "Keep" labels once, in the route header line, not on every strip.

## Sabotage blocks

Each sapper gets the same kind of block, below all the design blocks. A sapper has no spread, so it has one release tick and one dot.

- **Header:** sapper diamond icon and "Sapper · Bridge 1" (or "Sapper · Knock-out: Sniper"), 16 px bold. Then in 12 px grey: "leaves 6.0 s · reaches target 12.4 s".
- **Spread track:** a single 24 px knob at the release time. Drag it to change when the sapper leaves.
- **Path strip:** a single dot for the sapper, plus a marker for its target: a "B" on the bridge tile, or a small tower icon where the target tower's range starts.
- **Drag the dot** to choose where the sapper is at T. Its release time follows, using the same rules and limits as groups.
- **"Reaches target"** time comes from the ghost wave. If the sapper never gets there (for example, the route no longer passes the tile), show "won't reach target" in red instead.
- Show sabotage blocks only in rounds that allow sabotage (2–7), as today.

## How-to-use button

A clearly visible button at the top right of the Wave step opens short instructions. It replaces today's grey help sentence at the bottom.

- **Button:** a pill with an outlined "?" icon and the words "How to use", 12 px caps, accent-coloured outline. Always visible, never hidden behind a menu.
- **First visit per device:** the panel opens by itself once. After that, only when pressed.
- **Panel:** opens over the Wave step, with a "Got it" button and a close ×. Tapping outside also closes it.
- Use this text exactly:

```
Plan your wave

1. Press play.
   Watch your units walk the route. Drag the big slider to jump to any moment.

2. Choose WHEN units leave (top track of each group).
   Drag the left knob to set when the first unit leaves.
   Drag the right knob to set when the last unit leaves.
   Close together = a tight bunch. Far apart = a long line.
   Drag the middle to move the whole group earlier or later.

3. Choose WHERE they are (thin strip under it).
   Stop the big slider at a moment, say 12 s.
   Drag the group along the strip to where you want it at 12 s.
   The game works out when it has to leave.

4. Line them up.
   Every strip is the same route, left = entry, right = keep.
   Put groups at the same spot, or one just behind another.

5. Sappers work the same way.
   Drag a sapper to where you want it, and it leaves at the right time.

All units must leave between 0 s and 20 s.
If a group won't move further, that's the limit.
Frost and the defender's changes can still slow you down.
```

## Text hierarchy and tests

Use exactly these levels so the eye goes master panel → group names → details. Keep the game's current fonts and colours.

| Level | Used for | Size and style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | T readout ("T = 17.2 s") | 28 px bold, white |
| 2 | Step title "WAVE PLAN" | 18 px bold caps, letter-spaced |
| 3 | Group and sapper names | 16 px bold, the design's colour |
| 4 | Buttons (presets, How to use) | 12 px caps, outlined |
| 5 | Group details, "busiest second", route length label | 12 px, grey |
| 6 | Axis labels (0 s, Entry, Keep) | 11 px, dim grey |

Rename the title "WAVE TIMELINE 20 seconds" to "WAVE PLAN": the timeline now covers more than the 20 s release.

### Tests

1. Play runs T from its current value to 60 s at real speed, then stops; pressing play at 60 s restarts from 0 s.
2. Dragging the master slider, a knob, a band or a path strip pauses playback.
3. Space bar toggles play/pause; leaving the Wave step pauses.
4. Both knobs are drawn on every design's spread track and each moves only its own end.
5. Spread tracks and path strips all share the same left and right x positions.
6. A group whose front and rear are 10 cells apart on a 20-cell route fills half its path strip.
7. Dragging a sapper dot changes its release time; its header shows "reaches target" or "won't reach target".
8. The How to use panel opens once on first visit, then only from the button.
