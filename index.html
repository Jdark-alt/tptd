Two Player Tower Defence · v20.2

How to play

DefenderWalls and one tunable tower. Hold the keep through every wave.
AttackerDesign an army, send sappers, time the wave. Break the keep.
Keep125 points a wave, plus 25 for every wave under 8. Whatever gets through comes off it.
  1. Defender, in secret. Build: walls bend the route, towers shoot it, shapes drop in ready-made pieces. Tap a tower to tune it. From round 2, Test plays last wave’s army against your build. Ready.
  2. Attacker, in secret. Army: pick units. Sabotage, if you want it: walls to bridge and towers to knock out, each carried by a sapper. Wave: time units and sappers on a 20-second track, and Test it against the defense you saw. Ready.
  3. The wave. Everything is revealed and plays out. Tap a unit or tower to inspect it, and replay the wave from the summary.
  4. The split. Each round adds a 200-coin pot. The more gets through, the bigger the defender’s share, from 30% to 70%.

Play on one device and take turns, online on two, or against the computer; pick how many waves the match lasts at the start (online the defender’s pick counts). Each side has 3 minutes to plan each round; when the clock runs out, the plan goes as it stands. Tap to build and drag to draw walls; on touch screens a tower takes a second tap to place. Drag a tower to move it, or onto a wall or another tower to swap them, free. The + beside a slider is what the next step costs.

Hints (on by default, switch beside the build tabs): glowing tiles mark the walls that would lengthen the route most, and a tap on one places the whole set; the readout shows how long the average unit spends under fire.

Towers

One tower type. Its tuning starts at 40 coins and every slider multiplies the price, so stacking upgrades on one tower costs more than spreading them. Fire rate is free. Each tower also pays a build fee of 10 for every tower you already own. Removing a tower refunds its tuning and the top fee, and dragging one to a new cell is free.

40 × range × damage × accuracy × splash × frost × poison × chain + fee

Accuracy. Every tower starts at 80%. The Accuracy slider raises it to 87, 94 or 100% (×1.15, ×1.35, ×1.6), and fire rate shifts each shot: heavy shots hit more often, rapid fire less.

Shots per second
Accuracy

Each shot rolls once. A hit works as always: armor, splash, effects. A miss does nothing and applies no effects, but a splash miss still blasts the units around its target, just not the target. Chain only jumps from a hit, and its jumps always land.

Army

Up to four permanent designs; units that die come back next wave. Health, speed, armor, regeneration and resistance multiply the price, and above 60 coins a surcharge applies, so one giant unit costs more than its parts. Regeneration only heals out of fire, and resistances cut one kind of damage by 60%.

15 × health × speed × armor × regen × resist

On the wave plan each design is a block. Its track covers the 20 seconds when units can leave, one dot per unit: drag its knobs to set when the first and the last unit leave. Bunched units overwhelm single-target towers; fewer, tougher units in a looser wave beat splash.

Above the blocks, a big time slider previews the wave on the defense you saw: scrub to any moment and every unit and sapper shows where it will be. Under each track, a strip for the route shows where that group is then. Drag the group along it and its release time moves so it’s there at that moment; line the strips up to make groups meet.

Route and terrain

Units take the cheapest route: a step per cell, plus half a step for every tower that can hit it. They walk around tower range when the detour is short, so walls are how you force them through fire. A tower knocked out mid-wave still counts, so a knock-out never changes the route. The dashed line always shows the route.

Every match gets a new map. Rough ground (dotted, about 5%) is open to units but nothing can be built on it. Rock (hatched, about 5%) blocks like a free wall and can’t be removed, built on or bridged.

Sabotage

Each target gets a sapper that marches with the army. It walks the route like any unit, towers shoot it like any unit, and it only does its job if it gets there: shot down, its coins are lost. It does nothing at the keep. Time each sapper on the wave track; units walking ahead of it draw the fire.

Bridge. Goes on a wall you’ve seen beside the route you saw, or beside one of your bridges. Its sapper lays it on reaching the marked tile beside the wall: the wall’s coins go back to the defender and the cell is open ground for the rest of the match; nobody can build there again. If the route no longer passes that tile, or a tower stands where the wall was, it misses. From 25 coins, at most 1 a wave.

Knock-out. Goes on a tower you’ve seen. Its sapper stops it shooting for the rest of the wave the moment it walks into the tower’s range; units keep their route. It hits the spot: a tower moved away is safe. It costs 15 plus 20% of the tower’s slider price (its tuning, not its build fee), and the towers you target in one wave can add up to 50% of the slider prices of all the towers you saw; one tower always fits.

Each bridge costs 25% of its base more for every bridge you sent in earlier waves, and each knock-out 25% more for every earlier knock-out, landed or not. Targets in the same wave don’t raise each other’s price.

No sabotage in the first or the final wave.
